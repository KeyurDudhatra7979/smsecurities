# S.M. Securities V12 — Executive White Premium

## Changes
- Preserved original business identity, orange bull/shield logo, contact details, service and legal content.
- Added `v12-premium.css` as a separate maintainable premium theme layer; `styles.css` remains for backward compatibility.
- Updated homepage headline, introductory copy, CTAs, trust-oriented non-statistical hero messages.
- Introduced a skip-to-content link, improved focus visibility, and reduced favicon size.
- Added navy/white/orange executive visual language across hero, navigation, cards, contact, and footer.

## Before publishing
1. Review and verify all service claims, registration/partner disclosures, office hours and legal pages with qualified advisors.
2. Run `python prepare_deploy.py --help` and follow `DEPLOYMENT_CHECKLIST.md` to configure your production domain, canonical URLs and sitemap.
3. Test desktop/mobile rendering and WhatsApp enquiry on real devices.
4. Verify Google Maps office location and business contact information.
5. `styles.css` is retained; consolidate its legacy overrides in a future refactor after visual regression testing.

Open `index.html` locally to preview. The original V11 pages and scripts are preserved.
